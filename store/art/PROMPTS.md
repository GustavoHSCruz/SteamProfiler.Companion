# Generated art plates

Both source plates were generated with Codex's built-in image generation tool.
They contain no product text or product UI. `store/make.mjs` adds the real
SteamProfiler typography, mark and browser captures.

## `library-data-wide.png`

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: master promotional background for a browser-extension store marquee and small tile
Primary request: create a premium cinematic abstract visualization of a PC game library becoming useful data: a field of dark floating rectangular game cards and compact metric bars, with one warm amber card glowing as the focal point; the scene should suggest discovery, comparison, live statistics, and personal play history without depicting a literal app screen
Input images: the previous marquee is a style and palette reference only; the product screenshot is a UI reference only for the visual language of compact data cards, not an edit target
Scene/backdrop: near-black layered space with deep graphite panels, subtle grid depth, faint amber atmospheric glow
Subject: elegant floating game-card slabs and small data blocks, arranged into a coherent library constellation; one hero amber card with light traveling into nearby analytical blocks
Style/medium: polished high-end 3D editorial render, restrained, tactile matte materials, subtle glass and metallic edges, believable depth, not generic sci-fi
Composition/framing: extremely wide panoramic 5:2 composition; visual interest concentrated on the right two-thirds; the left third must remain calm, dark, and uncluttered for brand typography; all important shapes must survive a center crop to 11:7 for a small tile
Lighting/mood: controlled warm amber rim light against deep black, subtle volumetric haze, sophisticated and confident
Color palette: #0b0a0e, #131219, #282631, #4a4458, #ffb454, #c97f22, small restrained off-white highlights
Materials/textures: matte graphite, smoked glass, fine brushed metal, soft amber emissive surfaces, very light film grain
Constraints: no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no recognizable trademarks, no Steam logo, no controller, no keyboard, no people, no watermark; preserve generous clean negative space on the left; do not reproduce the reference layout literally
Avoid: neon cyberpunk, blue lighting, purple-dominant palette, clutter, busy HUD, stock-photo look, fantasy game imagery, fake UI text
```
